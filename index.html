About Voltiva

A wholesale distributor built by former buyers, for the people still doing the buying.

Our Story

We got tired of chasing our own orders

Voltiva started in a Charlotte warehouse in 2019, founded by three people who had spent a combined twenty years buying consumer goods for retailers and online sellers. They knew exactly what was broken: phantom inventory, list pricing on reorders, and authenticity paperwork that took a week to arrive.

So they built the opposite. Voltiva only lists what is physically on a shelf. Pricing tiers are published up front. Sourcing documentation is attached to the lot, not emailed on request. And every account gets a named rep instead of a ticket number.

Seven years later we run eighteen warehouses across five regions and serve more than nineteen hundred trade accounts — but the rule has not changed. If it says it ships today, it ships today.

What We Stand For

Wholesale Program

From a 50-unit case to a full container load — tiered pricing, dedicated support, and freight that lands when you need it.

Order Sizing

From one case to full container loads

Most categories start at a 50 to 100 unit minimum. Larger electronics such as TVs start at 25 units, and pallet-priced lines are quoted per pallet with project pricing.

Four published tiers move on trailing 90-day volume. You don't apply for a tier and you don't renegotiate one — the account moves when the volume does, and the new pricing applies to the next order automatically.

  • Tier 1 — under $5,000 / mo · standard wholesale
  • Tier 2 — $5,000–$25,000 / mo · 4–7% below standard
  • Tier 3 — $25,000–$100,000 / mo · 8–13% below, freight allowances
  • Tier 4 — $100,000+ / mo · project pricing, dedicated stock allocation
