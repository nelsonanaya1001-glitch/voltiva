§2 — Fulfillment Path

From application to dock, in order

Every account and every order moves through the same six-stage line — no shortcuts, no phantom stock.

01

Account Verification

Business license and resale certificate checked; accounts go live within 48 hours.

02

Catalog & Pricing Access

Tiered wholesale pricing unlocks by trailing volume — no quote requests needed.

§4 — Account Terms

Built for trade accounts, not marketplace traffic

No consumer storefront, no MAP leakage — Voltiva sells wholesale only.

01

Verified accounts only

Resale certificate and business license required before pricing is visible — never a consumer checkout.

02

Compliance-checked sourcing

UL- or ETL-listed lines only, with documentation on file for permitting and inspection.

Your rep knows gauge, voltage, and code requirements — not just SKUs and shipping dates.

Volume-tier pricing

Pricing steps down automatically as trailing order volume climbs — no renegotiation required.

MAP-protected lines

Pull live stock, pricing, and tracking straight into your own systems — no manual CSV exports.
