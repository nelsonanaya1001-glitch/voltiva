Our Story

We got tired of chasing our own orders

Voltiva started in a Charlotte warehouse in 2019, founded by three people who had spent a combined twenty years buying electrical gear for contractors and resellers. They knew exactly what was broken: phantom inventory, list pricing on reorders, and spec sheets that took a week to arrive.

So they built the opposite. Voltiva only lists what is physically on a shelf. Pricing tiers are published up front. Documentation is attached to the SKU, not emailed on request. And every account gets a named rep instead of a ticket number.

Seven years later we run eighteen warehouses across five regions and serve more than nineteen hundred trade accounts — but the rule has not changed. If it says it ships today, it ships today.

Wholesale Program

From a 10-unit reorder to a full container load — tiered pricing, dedicated support, and freight that lands when you need it.

Order Sizing

From 10 units to full container loads

Most lines start at a 10 to 50 unit minimum. Tools and accessories go lower; DC fast charging, transformers, and utility-scale storage are quoted per unit with project pricing.

Four published tiers move on trailing 90-day volume. You don't apply for a tier and you don't renegotiate one — the account moves when the volume does, and the new pricing applies to the next order automatically.

  • Tier 1 — under $5,000 / mo · standard wholesale
  • Tier 2 — $5,000–$25,000 / mo · 4–7% below standard
  • Tier 3 — $25,000–$100,000 / mo · 8–13% below, freight allowances
  • Tier 4 — $100,000+ / mo · project pricing, dedicated stock allocation
